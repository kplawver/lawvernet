<p>Study finds women more likely to apply to job ads with precise qualifications. For one vague ad, 6% of qualified women applied vs 22% of men. With more precise lang, 29% of women applied. In another, 42% of women applied vs 56% of men; precise ad=62% of women.</p><p>This gives some empirical support to the oft-quoted stat that men apply for a job when they meet 60% of the qualifications, but women apply only if they meet 100% of them.<br><a href="https://womensagenda.com.au/business/vague-language-in-job-ads-can-push-women-away-new-research-shows-how-employers-can-attract-more-female-applicants/" rel="nofollow noopener" translate="no" target="_blank"><span class="invisible">https://</span><span class="ellipsis">womensagenda.com.au/business/v</span><span class="invisible">ague-language-in-job-ads-can-push-women-away-new-research-shows-how-employers-can-attract-more-female-applicants/</span></a></p>
