You know how you read something really great, but after a while it slowly slips away and you forget all about it? That's happened to me and [Bone](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0963660942/qid=1034098105/sr=2-1/ref=sr_2_1/104-8425806-9307109). It's this amazing comic by Jeff Smith, full of wonder, humor action and amazing characters and it just slipped out the backdoor of my mind without so much as a good-bye.

I think Max would love it... maybe for Christmas (shhhh, don't tell him).
