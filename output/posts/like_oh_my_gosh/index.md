My [adorable sister](http://www.dprophet.com/meggers/2002_09_01_archives.html#80984218) is going to play a valley girl in a one act play. I can't wait to see it. If there's one thing my sister isn't (and I'm not surprised there's only one thing, for she is so many things), it's a valley girl. It will show her true acting talent. That, and it will make me laugh until I wet myself.
