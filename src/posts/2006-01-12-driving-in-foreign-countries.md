I've never driven in a foreign country. When we drove around Ireland, I made Porter do all the driving. Now, I'm going to France for the Plenary, and I'm renting a car. The only thing that's making me less jittery about doing so is having [a friend](http://glazman.org) who lives there and is feeding me driving tips for driving on the Riviera like:

- Traffic coming from the right has right-of-way unless they have a solid white line or dashed white line.
- Always lock your doors while driving
- No turning on red
- France now has speed cameras, and they're not friendly.
