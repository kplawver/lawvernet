After our marathon meeting this morning, the folks in my group headed over to [Banjara](http://www.banjaracuisine.com) for some Indian food. Why? I suggested it because Jen doesn't like Indian food and I had a terrible jones for it. I'd never been there before, and I was pleasantly surprised. The lunch buffet is stocked with really good stuff. Their Tandoori Chicken is the best I've ever had, and the other dishes were excellent. There were four main dishes on the buffet, Tandoori Chicken, a salad, six different chutneys, four or five appetizers and a lovely garbanzo bean salad. It was all excellent. If you're ever in Ashburn, VA and need some Indian food, definitely check them out.

And, without caffeine, and with a buffet in me - I'm sleeeee-eeeepy.
