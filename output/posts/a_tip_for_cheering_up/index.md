Need another pick-me-up? Go listen to some old **They Might Be Giants**, or watch their documentary: **Gigantic (A Tale Of Two Giants)**. It shows up on IFC every month or so. It's funny, and will make you smile.\\ You can't listen to any song on **Flood**, **Don't Let's Start** or **Subliminal** and _not_ smile. It's a physical impossibility.
