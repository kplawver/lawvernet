You'd think that if you fax someone something, the fax was received, and nothing further was said that everything is kosher, correct? I guess not. I just got another letter from the honorable and competant Virginia Office of Taxation saying we owe them \\$5000 for 1998, a year in which I don't think I travelled outside the state of Arizona, and am pretty sure I lived in Tucson. I mean, my employer says I was employed in Tucson and didn't transfer to Virginia until February of 1999. I faxed them a signed letter from the head of the HR department stating that fact. It doesn't take much investigation to see that my address was 373 N Wilmot Drive in Tucson, Arizona until February of '99.\\ I know they're just people doing their job, but dammit, do it right! I'm tired of dealing with this when it's obviously their error and if they expended any energy at all, they'd know it too. It doesn't take much thought, people!
