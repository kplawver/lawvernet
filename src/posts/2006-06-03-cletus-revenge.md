I went to Target this morning and got some clippers, because after two weeks of growth, I wasn't using disposable razors to take care of it again. Cletus, among others, made an appearance. You've got the [Porter](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kplawver/159592346/), the [Beefeater](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kplawver/159592470/) and of course, a [couple](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kplawver/159592539/) [Cletus](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kplawver/159592904/).

And when that was done and I properly shaved, I gave myself a haircut! I grabbed the number 3 guard and did the whole thing. Why? First, it's summer. Second, I just didn't want to have to deal with making an appointment and going to get it cut. The real reason? Simplicity. No more brushing, simple wash, dry and go. Simple is good.
