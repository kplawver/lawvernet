[Composer](http://mozilla.org/products/mozilla1.x/ "Mozilla 1.6 home page") is usually pretty painful to work with, but I realized today that it's my old laptop's fault. Composer works like a charm on my swanky new G5 (oh yes, I did not tell you the story of the swanky new G5 - it will have to wait). Composer's extremely responsive in redrawing tables and other clumsy elements when it has two, count 'em, two 2ghz G5's to do the calculations. And no, I'm not using Composer to write anything that's going to see the light of day. It's a word processor replacement to update a requirements document (using a template I **have** to use). I hate rules.
