Huge congratulations are in order for Tim Kaine, who won the Virginia governor's office convincingly. But, more important to Loudoun county, ~~both [Bruce Roemmelt](http://www.electroemmelt.org/) and~~ [Dave Poisson](http://www.poisson4delegate.com/) won!!! I don't know what this means for trends, or projections for 2006, but a big thank you to the [Loudoun County Democrats](http://portal.lcdems.com/). I'm sorry I couldn't help more this time. **UPDATE** - In my drug-induced haze, it looks like I spoke too soon and Bruce actually lost. Sorry, Bruce!!!
