> "Dad, don't ramen it."
> 
> Brian Lawver

That's how my son tells me not to overdo it. Why? Well, I had a thing about making [overcomplicated ramen recipes](https://lawver.net/2017/05/southern-pork-ramen-broth/), and now it's a thing.

I'm not going to ramen Thanksgiving this year! It's a good year to keep it simple, play the hits, and have some fun. What's the menu? Glad you asked!

Pregaming:

- Pimento cheese

- Fancy crackers

- Relish/veggie tray

- Deviled eggs

Main: Turkey & gravy

Sides:

- Brussel sprouts

- Mashed potatoes

- [Korean corn cheese](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/corn-cheese)

- Dressing

- Green bean casserole

Dessert:

- Pumpkin pie

- French silk pie

Ok, that seems like a lot, but most of it is just assembly and something we've made before. This is Brian's first year of being _fully involved_ in the cooking, which is exciting.

And that's it! What's going to be on your table tomorrow?
