<p>Story of loveliness for you all because you've been so well behaved recently.</p><p>I dated an Italian guy for about 3 months six years ago, and my mum decided it was time to start learning Italian.</p><p>The relationship didn't last but my mum never stopped learning Italian by herself in retirement. She's never had a teacher or been to a class; she's taught herself from apps and lots of books I've thrown at her. </p><p>She speaks Italian with the strongest English accent you've ever heard. There's no doubt to anyone that she is not a native speaker.</p><p>So last week she went to Turin for a couple of days, and she came back *buzzing*. She's been to Italy before and spoken Italian but this time was different. </p><p>She was having conversations in Italian with everyone about everything. Basic conversations but she was doing it and loving it. </p><p>In one restaurant the waiter couldn't possibly believe that she was an English person speaking this language. English?! English people only speak English! What the actual?! He was so overjoyed at her efforts that he kept bringing her all sorts of free food and limoncello until she asked him to stop.</p><p>When I spoke to her a couple of days ago she said it's so weird that you sit at home and learn this language but it doesn't mean anything. It's all nonsense.
