I've been writing [ficlets](http://ficlets.com) at night after work as a way to unwind and do something creative outside of writing code. It's been fun! I've been trying to write sequels to other peoples' stories as an exercise in trying other styles, genres and voices. I think I'd call the results mixed, but fun.

Here are this week's:

- [The Devil in a Shop Window](http://ficlets.com/stories/4235) - I seem to do tragedy pretty well. This is a sequel to a story about alchoholism and hitting rock bottom. The original provided a perfect setup.
- [Shokran Gazillan](http://ficlets.com/stories/4398) - I had to do _research_ for this one (looking up "thank you" in Arabic). It's a sequel to a story about a soldier in Iraq on patrol stumbling on a wounded Iraqi child and his brother.
- [Basil, Rock God](http://ficlets.com/stories/4402) - A sequel to a hilarious story about an accountant who wakes up in the body of a rock star. Lots of questions to answer, but all I wanted to write about were leather pants and his stupid non-rock star name.
- [Keeping the Peace? What Peace?](http://ficlets.com/stories/4581) - I re-read **Shokran Gazillan** and had to write a sequel to figure out what happened next.
- [God's Fist](http://ficlets.com/stories/4583) - A sequel to a great **Deep Impact**~~style story about an impending comet impact. Reminded me a lot of [the first ficlet (oddly enough called **The End**)](http://ficlets.com/stories/1) (which has spawned a surprising number of sequels~~ it's actually got a bunch of threads coming off of it!).

Jen came downstairs earlier and asked me if I was working (before I started writing, I was checking on stats, checking for reported stories, and wander). I gave her a blank look... was I? She laughed at me and told me it was OK if I was working. I shook my head and told her I was playing with my "toys".
