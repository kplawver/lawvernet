A couple weeks ago, I helped my [sister](http://heathershow.com) with a little movie project that she filmed all on her digital camera. The day I dressed Brian up in his manly overalls and flannel shirt, I took a little movie of him playing. The voice is me trying to get him to make noise. Of course, Little Wrestler Brian didn't come out and roar, but he's still cute.
