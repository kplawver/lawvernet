<p>The season finale of Somebody Somewhere hit me like a train. We have people for so little time, and don’t get to choose when they leave our lives, whether they’re family, friends, lovers or coworkers. The best we can do is celebrate the time we have, as fiercely and with as much loving kindness as possible. When it’s over, we treasure the memories, mend our broken hearts, and then keep on loving.  It never ends, and losing people doesn’t get easier, but we have to keep loving.</p>
