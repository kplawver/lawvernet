I'm not the only one who thinks he's brilliant. [My sister](http://www.dprophet.com/meggers/2002_06_02_archives.html#77314325) tells a cute little story about my boy. He's so smart, it's scary. We've talked to enough people about his spelling and other reading-like capabilities that we know he's really reading now. The scary part is, he's really reading. He's figured it out himself, mostly, which is the scariest part. He's the best.

He was so cute this morning. I should have taken a picture. He ran into our room in his little running shorts wearing the funny blue cowboy hat [Jen](/jen) got him and singing. He wasn't wearing a shirt, and he was just too cute for words.
