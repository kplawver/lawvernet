I stayed up way too late last night having fun with my SxSW pals [Kevin Smokler](http://kevinsmokler.com), [Dave Thomas](http://daviddylanthomas.com) and [James McNally](http://consolationchamps.com). I ended up driving us around to Bryan Busch's SxSW Karaoke Kick-Off (not the real title, but close). I didn't plan on singing anything, but I ended up doing a duet with [MD](http://mdnovember.com) of TMBG's **Don't Lets Start**, and with Dave on **Shock the Monkey**. The song of the night though, was Kevin and Dave's version of Queen's classic **Under Pressure**. I didn't get the whole thing, but here's the last minute:
