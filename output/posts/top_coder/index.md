![Top Coder 2006 sponsored by AOL](images/topcoder_banner.gif "Top Coder 2006 sponsored by AOL")\\ AOL is sponsoring the Top Coder Collegiate Challenge this year as part of the new [Developer Network](http://dev.aol.com). I've been involved in the discussion about the network since the beginning, and it's gratifying to see it finally bearing fruit. There is still a ton of work to do to make it into something we can all be proud of, but the seed is there, and it's off to a good start.\\ So, if you're into watching geeks under pressure (if you live with me, you've seen this already this month with [AIM Pages](http://aimpages.com)), you can watch the live cast this Friday at 4:30EST over on the [Developer Network](http://dev.aol.com).
