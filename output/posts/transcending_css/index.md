If you can wait until October, you should get [Transcending CSS](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0321410971/ref=ase_andallthatmal-20/102-0275249-5930531?n=283155&tagActionCode=andallthatmal-20) . [Andy](http://stuffandnonsense.co.uk) showed me some of it at WWW2006, and it's _really_ cool. There are only a few designery standardsy books I own, including the [orange bible](http://zeldman.com/dwws) and [Dan's second book](http://www.simplebits.com/publications/bulletproof/) and **The Zen of CSS Design**. This one will join 'em on the shelf.
