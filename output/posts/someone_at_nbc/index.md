Someone at NBC loves [Snatch](http://us.imdb.com/Title?0208092). Have you been watching the NBA playoffs on NBC? I have, and every time they cut to a stat graphic (Shaq has X free throw attempts per game per blah, blah, blah) they play a snippet of a different song off the **Snatch** soundtrack. It's pretty funny. The first time I noticed it was when they played the theme song (called Diamond - track #2) during Kings/Lakers Game 5. They've been working their way through the CD. I'm keeping an ear out for **Massive Attack** Wednesday night.

Oh, and what do you think of the new colors? I'm going to play with moving things around when I get some free time.
