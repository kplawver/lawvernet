It's been a great year for actual music, but a sad one for me personally. Why? Rdio died. I _loved_ Rdio for years - even when I was working at a music startup building a competing service, I _still_ loved Rdio. And now it's gone. I tried Google Music, and had to give up on it because it lacks any idea of social, and does some very strange things with explicit lyrics. Now I'm on Spotify and it's all right. It's not perfect. It's definitely not Rdio - but it'll do.

Enough sadness, let's get on to the music! [Last year](http://lawver.net/2014/12/kevins-favorite-albums-of-2014/), I just threw together a list of the albums I liked and put them in three categories. That was a cop out and saved me from having to pick a _single album_. This year, I'm picking a single album that is my favorite of the year! And then a couple more than I really liked.

My album of the year is...

[Sometimes I Sit and Think and Sometimes I Just Sit](https://open.spotify.com/album/1WBps8cIMT6LYVq5rYh5ze) by Courtney Barnett! It's funny. It's fun to sing along with, and the songs are actually about things. This album came out early on this year and I kept going back to it all year.

It was really hard to pick just one album but I did it. Courtney's biggest competition was from an artist that my friend Bryan told me about - **Ghostpoet**. His album, [Shedding Skin](https://open.spotify.com/album/10eqXjng3u6BvyZGiLph5w), is fantastic. It reminds me a lot of **Massive Attack**, which is _always_ a good thing. You should listen to it, a lot.

There were a _lot_ of really good albums that came out this year. So many that my [Kevin's 2015 Favorites](https://open.spotify.com/user/kplawver/playlist/3MJjSJPWngTdUpwJQP6Bq0) playlist has 340 songs in it and if you listened to it all at once, it would take 23 hours and 29 minutes. So, get started!
