As of 8:54 EST this morning, [nonDependant](http://www.nondependant.com) is live to the world. I'm not sure what will happen, how fast or who will join up, if anyone. I don't know that I'm really all concerned about it. Of course, I hope it's successful, but it feels good to have just made the decision to do something and to have put the effort into doing it.

I hope you'll check it out, and if you want, join us. If you don't, that's fine too. It's an experiment. I don't know what it will become, and right now, I honestly can't tell you even what it is past the fact that it's running [Drupal](http://www.drupal.org), has a lamb in the bottom right corner of the page that I spent an evening outlining in Photoshop, and has several hundred lines of prose that [Dawson](http://www.norcalblog.com/mtdr) and I slaved over. What happens now really isn't up to either of us...
