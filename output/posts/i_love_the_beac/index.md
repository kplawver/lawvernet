I told my MIL that the kid and I could share a birthday cake (Spiderman!), cuz I don't really care about the cake, but that I would pick the dinner menu (pot roast and mashed potatoes, yum), cuz Brian doesn't really care about the dinner. But we'd both get presents, yay presents! It was a good plan. But then my MIL surprised me with a cake of my own anyway! Wheee.\\ [![Photobucket](images/2503138523_671f53ee4e.jpg)](http://s52.photobucket.com/albums/g23/jenjenhead/?action=view&current=2503138523_671f53ee4e.jpg)\\ It's a beach cake, woot! My MIL also got me a beach bag and sparkly flipflops. It was a theme!\\ While driving home, I told Max that he had an awesome Babba. (She made me a beach cake, y'all!) Brian popped up, "And me too.
