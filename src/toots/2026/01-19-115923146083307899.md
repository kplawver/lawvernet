<p>If you’re a white person, especially one who grew up in the US, and specifically in the South, and emphatically if you were raised Christian, I can’t recommend “Understanding and Dismantling Racism” by Joseph Barndt highly enough! (Sorry, originally fumble fingered that I couldn’t recommend it, but I can!)</p><p>It’s written with you in mind, and is about the gentlest introduction to the systems of racism and will help you get around the “immune response” we all have (that isn’t your fault - but we all need to learn to overcome). </p><p>And when you’re ready to go deep, “The Half Has Never Been Told” has, as they say, all the receipts. It is the most damning book I’ve ever read. It will change you, Southern or not (Yankees, you invented Jim Crow and profited the most from slavery, so put that smug away).</p>
