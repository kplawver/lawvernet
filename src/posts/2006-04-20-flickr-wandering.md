Whenever I'm stuck for something to do (like it's 7:45 on a Thursday morning, I'm stuck on the kitchen computer, and not awake enough to think straight), I like to wander Flickr. I usually start with the [most interesting photos of the last 7 days](http://www.flickr.com/explore/interesting/7days/) page, find someone I've not seen before, and then crawl through their photostream. Today, I found [a great portrait photographer](http://www.flickr.com/photos/darkshapesprowl/) who apparently lives in [Baltimore](http://www.flickr.com/photos/darkshapesprowl/tags/baltimore/). His portraits are funny and beautiful (there's one of a pug dog on a pink lawn chair that's priceless). He also has an [amazing photo of Muhammad Ali](http://www.darkshapesprowl.com/32105.php).

Sometimes, I try to find people from the same country. Right now, two of my favorite Flickr-tographers are [hkvam](http://www.flickr.com/photos/hkvam/) and [\_rebekka](http://www.flickr.com/photos/rebba/). They both have breathtaking landscape shots of Iceland. Oh, and I want [one of these.](http://www.flickr.com/photos/blueone/129167965/) (you can [buy some of them](http://www.kidrobot.com/search.php?keyword=dunny), and am wondering if Jen would ever [let me do this](http://www.flickr.com/photos/troymccluresf/130849312/).
