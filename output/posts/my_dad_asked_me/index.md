Only three more days until Paris! Two more days until my mom comes! Wheeee.\\ I spent a good portion of this morning cleaning out my fridge, in preparation of my mother's arrival. Plus, I can't get the vent-thingie at the bottom of the fridge clean. Booooo. Does that come off? Maybe I will try q-tips to get in there. Or Valium and a nap. Ha. The funniest thing about this situation is that our carpet is stained and the walls have crayon all over them, but I am fretting over fridge crannies.\\ Whenever I have problems like this, I am always tempted to take a picture to mail to my dad so he can talk me through to a solution.
