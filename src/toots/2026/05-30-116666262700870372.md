<p>The Game Changer board game kickstarter is super fun. Today’s challenge was to call the hotline and describe your favorite fake bird. Mine is, obviously, the Georgia Tufted Foghorn. It’s the tallest bird in the world at seven feet tall. The odd part is that it looks like a chicken, a normal sized chicken. So it’s about 12 inches of body and six feet of legs. </p><p>It’s aquatic and is usually seen standing in six feet of water, so most people just think it’s a chicken out for a float. </p><p>They get their name from the fact that they sound like a foghorn, and flocks of them are frequently heard in the low country marshland, annoying herons, alligators and other more regal wildlife.</p>
