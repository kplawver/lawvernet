---
title: "Savannah, oh, Savannah"
date: 2008-08-10
---

When I think about what I gave up to move down here (the list is long), I sometimes feel like crying. But there haven't been any tears yet! Well, at least not until last night when we were at a BBQ place and _they only had mustard-based BBQ sauce_. Apparently it is a _thing_ here, so it isn't a simple matter of finding a different BBQ restaurant. I mean, really![](images/?) Mustard-based BBQ sauce! I almost high-tailed it back to Virginia right then! I was mollified when Kevin (jokingly) suggested I could bring my own sauce next time. The boys and I are going up to Virginia before school starts. While there, I am going to stock up on individual packets of BBQ sauce from my favorite BBQ place. Oh yea.
