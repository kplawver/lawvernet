Today is Max's 9th birthday. To celebrate, I took the boys to the [Oatland Wildlife Center](http://www.oatlandisland.org) to see some alligators and other wildlife. Little did I realize that it was a two mile hike around the center to see everything. And we did the _whole_ thing. I, of course, took pictures which [are up on flickr if you want to go see the rest of them](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kplawver/sets/72157607739099926/).
