**Back to Music**: I brought in a bunch of old CDs today, and am now listening to my Living Colour collection (no, dammit, it's not the TV show). What amazing stuff. Between Corey Glover's stellar voice and Vernon Reid's guitar, they produced two of the best albums of the late 80's/early 90's. [Vivid](http://www.cdnow.com/cgi-bin/mserver/SID=1279989758/pagename=/RP/CDN/FIND/album.html/artistid=LIVING+COLOUR/itemid=317874) and [Time's Up](http://www.cdnow.com/cgi-bin/mserver/SID=1279989758/pagename=/RP/CDN/FIND/album.html/artistid=LIVING+COLOUR/itemid=318179) were just great. Other than **Nothingness**, Stain was a waste. But, then, [Vernon](http://www.cdnow.com/cgi-bin/mserver/SID=1279989758/pagename=/RP/CDN/FIND/album.html/artistid=REID*VERNON/itemid=362874) and [Corey](http://www.cdnow.com/cgi-bin/mserver/SID=1279989758/pagename=/RP/CDN/FIND/album.html/ArtistID=GLOVER*COREY/ITEMID=497594) came out with solo albums that redeemed them. All worth checking out. Oh laws yes (I read **The Stand** the weekend I bought **Time's Up** so that phrase is connected to the album for me).
