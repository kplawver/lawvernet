Ok, my favorite new feature in OS 10.2 has go to be [iChat](http://www.apple.com/macosx/jaguar/ichat.html). They've outdone the AIM client in almost every way. It's gorgeous in that iTunes kinda way, and the Rendezvous thing looks like it will be a lot of fun. The only problem is the fact that I think I'm the only person on my floor using it, and well, that means my Rendezvous window is empty. The thought baloons are cute, and I think might start to annoy me eventually. For now, they're a nice departure from the same old thing. Thankfully, you can shut them off and go back to formatting them as regular old IM's.
