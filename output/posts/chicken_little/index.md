Chicken Little, the new animated movie voiced by Zack Braff, is amusing, entertaining, and full of rockin' tunes, but man, do I hate it. The father constantly belittles and ignores Chicken Little and he ignores his coach's specific instruction, in order to gain glory. Kevin bought the movie 2 or 3 weeks ago thinking it would be a nice diversion for while the kids were sick. It worked. They love it. I think we've played the movie 2 or 3 _dozen_ times. But it still sucks.
