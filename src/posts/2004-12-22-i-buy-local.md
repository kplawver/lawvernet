I told you last week (or was it early this week) that I had more thoughts about buying local. Well, it's ready (and I did it at lunch today while eating chili). I present to you, my faithful friends and family: [I BUY LOCAL](http://ibuylocal.info). Right now, it's just that first post, but I'm hoping it blossoms into a group blog full of local business-supporting goodness. I've already got one blogger lined up to help me (when he "officially" joins up and posts something, I'll let ya know), and am looking for more.

It's not going to be a big thing, just a small thing that will hopefully help someone realize that where they get their goods and services from does make a difference. Thinking, even just a little bit, about who's affected by the money we put into the community is a start. I know I've just started.

If you're interested in helping out (we'll have stickers soon), let me know (kevin-at-ibuylocal.info).
