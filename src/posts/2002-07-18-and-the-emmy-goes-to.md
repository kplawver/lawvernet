The coolest thing about the [Emmy nomination list](http://www.cnn.com/2002/SHOWBIZ/TV/07/18/emmy02.emmy.nominations/index.html) for this year is that the nominations aren't dominated by crap actors who people think are "due", and there is a good collection of non-network shows and actors nominated. The fact that most of the cast of **Six Feet Under** was nominated, plus Michael Chiklis (who's show I haven't seen, but man, has FX ever gotten a nomination for anything?). It's a good sign I think that at least the Emmy's can grow up.
