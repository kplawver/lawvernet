I have a tv recommendation for you: _Undercover Boss_ at CBS.com. It's a reality show about the head of a major corporation doing entry-level work for a week. The episode I saw about Waste Management almost made me cry. It's shmaltzy and uplifting in the best way. I also saw the Hooters episode. It was fine, but I suggest watching the Waste Management episode instead. Run, don't walk, to watch this show!
