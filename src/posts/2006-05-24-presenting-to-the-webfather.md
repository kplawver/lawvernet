I just did my first presentation at [WWW](http://www2006.org) on [our microformat](http://developer.iamalpha.com/profile), and who was in the audience, but Tim Berners-Lee, the father of the web. There was a moment, sitting at the front of the room, waiting for my turn to present, that I got really nervous. I'm not normally nervous before I speak in front of a fairly small group (less than 100). This time, I was. I had a thirty minute presentation that I had to compress into 10, which didn't help.

I think Friday's will be easier. I get to talk about CSS, on stage with two people I know fairly well, and I have my full time. If you're at the conference, Friday's **Style and Layout** panel should be a lot of fun. I get to talk about the [guidelines](http://developer.iamalpha.com/module-best-practices#css) we set up for CSS in modules and themes in [AIM Pages](http://www.aimpages.com), and how that process has worked out for us so far.
