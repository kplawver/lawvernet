I mentioned [Chappelle's Show](http://www.comedycentral.com/tv_shows/chappellesshow/) last week in my [epic list of good tv](http://lawver.net/archive/002126.html), especially that I'd only seen the first episode and am reserving judgement BUT that it looks like it will be amazing. Well, folks, after watching last night's episode, I can honestly say I want to have Dave Chapelle's babies. The crackhead talking to school kids was almost as funny as the black white power sketch from last week. And the opening bit with the "pretty white girl" was just plain brilliant. Catch a repeat. Do whatever you have to to watch this show. You won't be sorry.
