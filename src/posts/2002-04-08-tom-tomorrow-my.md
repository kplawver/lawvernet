[Tom Tomorrow](http://www.salon.com/comics/tomo/2002/04/08/tomo/index.html), my Chevy Blazer and I hate you. You make so much sense, yet, I am somehow driven to now drive more and more, driving over mountain roads, spinning out in wet parking lots and for some odd reason I want to drive into the ocean to show my car's unique ability to navigate through wet sand and surf.
