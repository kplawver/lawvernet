The [ratings from this year's SxSW panels](http://2008.sxsw.com/blogs/ia.php/2008/05/08/p1250) are out and the panel I moderated, [Career Transitions: From DIY to Working for The Man](http://2008.sxsw.com/interactive/programming/panels_schedule/?action=show&id=IAP060356) was the 14th-ranked panel of the whole conference (out of 105)! We got a 4.55 out of 5 overall. It was a lot of fun and, of course, the panelists should get most of the credit: [Jason Garber](http://sixtwothree.org), [Leslie Jensen-Inman](http://morellc.com), [Cindy Li](http://cindyli.com), and [Thomas Vander Wal](http://vanderwal.net).\\ Now, I need to figure out what I'm doing next year....
