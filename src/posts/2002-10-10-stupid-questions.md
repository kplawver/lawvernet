I want to be able to slowly and methodically whoop anyone in the world. I'll start out with the old and decrepit, then move to the folks who fall into the unfortunate category, "fatter than me", and whoop on them with my fluid (molasses is a fluid) moves. Eventually, I'll be able to beat anyone, or at least bore them to death.
