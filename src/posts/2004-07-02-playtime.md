To sum up [Working out our Play Muscle](http://www.worthwhilemag.com/entry/2004/06/24/working_out_our_play_muscle.php "Worthwhile: Working out our Play Muscle"): play for thirty minutes a day. Yep, gotcha. How about play for eight, nine or ten hours every day? That's what me [being a pirate](/archive/2004/06/25/h09_im_a_pirate.php) is all about. I play almost all day, every day. A couple years ago, I figured out that work is a whole lot more manageable and livable if it's a game. I make up games during meetings, I turn stupid tasks into something subversive or silly. I make coding into a puzzle or a race. I see how much faster, better, smaller, neater, more complete I can make something than some mythical villain I pick out (look out, it may be **you**). I turn a gigantic task like getting an entire company to embrace web standards into a game of pirates...

I may be crazy, but it keeps me sane.
