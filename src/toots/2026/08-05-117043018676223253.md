<p>Last fall, I drove from Savannah to Denver and back to see Sigur Ros in concert (and to check some states off the list). I stopped in Kansas City and went to the Negro Leagues Baseball Museum.  The last exhibit is this replica diamond with statues of some of the greats in the middle of the game.</p><p>I&#39;ve been thinking about this the last week or so - not sure why.  Being there in person, especially when the museum is quiet, is haunting.  I felt like I was disturbing something.</p><p>So, if you ever find yourself in Kansas City, you should go.</p>
