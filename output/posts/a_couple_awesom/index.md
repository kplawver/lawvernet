Secondly, the awesome [Scott Andrew](http://scottandrew.com) is [self-producing his new album](http://www.saveyoufromyourself.com). I accidentally discovered Scott a _long_ time ago and loved his work with The Walkingbirds. I met him at [SxSW](http://sxsw.com), and he's a really good guy. He's [accepting donations to try to finish up the album](http://www.saveyoufromyourself.com), and I think that's great. It takes balls (there they are _again_!) to go it alone and try to do it all yourself, and I think that should not only be applauded, but supported. The more artists are able to really go it alone without having to sell their souls to corporate record labels, and make it work, that can only mean good things for music fans. It means more music for us, and gets us closer to the artists we like. Go, Scott, go!\\ I'm slowly working on the [Rails](http://rubyonrails.com) app that will power [The International Day of Awesomeness](http://dayofawesomeness.com) blog and an extra special something that my pal Kedar suggested. I've got things started, but nowhere near ready to launch - but keep your eyes peeled. I'm hoping to launch it in the next month or so...
