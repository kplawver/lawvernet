School's in session, which means the boys are bringing home all kinds of lovely viruses with them. We all have colds now (thanks, boys). What's the best thing for a cold? Chicken noodle soup! I decided I would take what I learned from my experiments with the [Momofuku Ramen recipe](http://luckypeach.com/recipes/momofuku-ramen-2-0/) and [crock pot ramen broth](http://lawver.net/2014/11/leftovers-accomplished-turkey-ramen/) and make a ramen-inspired but classic chicken noodle soup that will hopefully cure the common cold.

This is a two step soup. We first make a super concentrated fatty broth from chicken backs and vegetables in the crock pot overnight and then use that as the base for a more classic chicken soup.

### The Stock

[![A bunch of ingredients in the crockpot.](images/2015-08-29-18.37.31-1-620x620.jpg)](http://159.89.35.127/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/2015-08-29-18.37.31-1.jpg)

I used my 6 quart crockpot for this, but pretty much any crockpot that will fit all the ingredients in it and cover them with water will work.

#### Ingredients

- A bunch of chicken backs (I used 6) or a couple of chicken carcasses with some meat and fat left on them.
- Some olive oil
- 3-5 largish carrots, roughly chopped
- 2/3 of a head of celery, roughly chopped
- 1 large white onion, you guessed it, roughly chopped
- Some salt (2 teaspoons-ish)
- Some pepper (1 teaspoon-ish)
- Two healthy shakes of: dried rosemary, dried thyme, garlic and oregano.
- I also grabbed a bunch of fresh basil, chives and thyme from my garden, but it's up to you.

#### Directions

- Pre-heat your oven to 400 degrees
- Put the chicken backs (or carcasses) in a roasting pan, drizzle with olive oil, and shake some salt and pepper over them, and roast them in the oven for 30-45 minutes - until they're nice and brown.
- Take the chicken out of the oven and dump it and the resulting yummy liquid into the crock pot (carefully, it's really hot).
- Cover the chicken with the vegetables and herbs.
- Cover all of _that_ with water and leave on low overnight.
- In the morning, strain the contents of the crockpot through a fine mesh colander and put it in a stock pot. I ended up with about a quart, maybe two, of the super concentrated stock (taste it, but be careful, because it might blow your socks off).

### The Soup

[![chicken soup in the pot, ready to be skimmed](images/2015-08-30-11.52.58-620x620.jpg)](http://159.89.35.127/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/2015-08-30-11.52.58.jpg)

I used a medium-ish stock pot for this part. It needs to be able to hold about 4 quarts of liquid plus all the solid ingredients.

#### Ingredients

- The stock from the night before.
- A bunch of boneless chicken thighs, chopped up.
- A white onion finely chopped
- The rest of that head of celery, finely chopped (whatever size you want to eat it in the soup)
- 2-3 largish carrots, you guessed it, finely chopped.
- I also added some okra from the garden, but that's totally up to you.
- I also added some chopped up kale near the end, because it's healthy and stuff.
- Medium egg noodles
- Salt and pepper to taste

#### Directions

- Put the stock pot on the stove on low with the stock from the night before.
- Add 2 quarts of water
- Add the chicken, onion, celery, carrots and okra.
- Simmer all day or until you can't wait anymore, skimming off some of the scum and fat as it goes. Check on it every hour or so and give it a stir.
- Boil the medium egg noodles per their directions and put them in the bottom of each of the bowls you're going to serve the soup in (remember, this is ramen-inspired, plus, no one likes soggy limp noodles). You could also chop up some green onions and throw them in there.
- Ladle in some of the soup, salt and pepper to taste and eat it!
