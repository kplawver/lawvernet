I told you [how it happened a while ago](/archive/2005/07/07/h14_the_boot_is_not_made_for_walking.php). Now, I'll tell you what I did. I just found [this great explanation of what an accessory navicular is](http://footandankle.mdmercy.com/conditions/flatfeet/navicular.html). I've got one of those, and my little stumble in India moved mine around where it's not supposed to be. I've got illustrations for you too:

**A Normal Accessory Navicular**:

![](images/navicular.png)

**My Accessory Navicular**:

![](images/my_navicular.png)

If you're not sure what you're looking at. The toes are right above the picture. The left side is the instep. What this all means is that I'm in a good deal of pain because this little "extra" bone is now screwing up the whole rest of my foot by moving the large tendon that controls the arch. That's causing the arch to collapse, which is making other bones go where they don't belong.\\ We've been trying the "rest" bit of the treatment plan for a while now (three months and counting), and I'm just about done (finished, finito, finis, kaput) with it. This cast sucks. And, if I have surgery (wait, not if, when...), I'll be in a cast for **six more weeks**. Merry fother-mucking Christmas.

I've done this before with my right knee (twice) and my left ankle (left). I'm not sure why this time is worse. It just is. It's probably worse because it was such a stupid nothing injury. It's worse because I didn't think it was anything when I did it, and it's turned out to be _something_.
