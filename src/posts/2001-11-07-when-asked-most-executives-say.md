[When asked, most executives say they're not going to be moving to Linux in the near future.](http://news.cnet.com/news/0-1003-200-7803522.html?tag=mn_hd) That's funny. What "executive" really knows what OS their web site or infrastructure runs on? Do they care? Here, we're moving quite a few "essential" systems to Linux, and folks are loving it. The best part is the move didn't come from an "executive". The idea was brought up by developers and sysadmins. They proved the case for it, and did it. Gotta love that.
