Is anyone else annoyed at the combo movie/basketball ads showing on TNT during playoff games? Not only are they lame tie-ins for the movies, they're super lame promos for playoff basketball, which really doesn't need a whole lot of help.\\ And as an added bonus, they're some of the most forced, weak and painful stretched metaphors for the playoffs I've ever seen. They're so bad they make Bill Walton's frequent hyperbole and crap pontificating seem almost palatable.\\ TNT, come on. Charles Barkley + Jeremy Irons + Crusades + Detroit != Compelling Advertising. Get a clue.
