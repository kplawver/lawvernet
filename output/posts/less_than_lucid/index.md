I'm fighting a wicked sinus infection. Jen and Max are at church, and I'm sitting here with the laptop, wrapped up in my blanket, watching **Meet the Press** and reading my favorite blogs.

I've been thinking recently about why personal journals and blogs fascinate me. For me, it's the chance to get a glimpse into lives of people I'd never meet otherwise. Whether it's a [freelance translator in France](http://www.openbrackets.com), a [college librarian in Texas](http://www.chatterwaul.com/oddfellow/chatter.html), a [single web designer in Minneapolis](http://www.iwilldare.com) or any of the other dozen and a half blogs I check out on a mostly daily basis, I enjoy seeing the pieces of their lives they share through their sites. Most of them are much better writers than I am, and have no problem sharing their feelings on the topics they discuss.

These people are strangers. They're strangers who in a lot of cases I consider friends, even though we've never met or done more than exchange a couple e-mails. It's another strange witness to the power and mystery of interpersonal interaction on the internet.

I'm going to go make some hot chocolate and lay down. I can't stop coughing, and I think my face is going to pop.
