[CIA Holds Terror Suspects in Secret Prisons](http://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/content/article/2005/11/01/AR2005110101644.html "CIA Holds Terror Suspects in Secret Prisons")\\ How do we justify this? If this is against the law, both US law, and treaties we've agreed to as a country, and the President can't sign a "finding" that breaks the law - how were we able to do this? Why didn't someone in the government put a stop to it?\\ Are we so far gone as a country, so without ethics, so without a moral compass, that we're not all outraged by this? We're in the business of _torture_.\\ I'm ashamed of my government. It's time for us, as a nation, to take a long hard look at ourselves, and how our government represents us.\\ We should demand better. There are solutions to terrorism and to the other problems that plague us that don't require torture and don't require us to turn against our better nature.\\ Shame on us for not demanding better. Shame on us for re-electing the fools who brought us here. Shame on us and Congress and our courts for not holding them accountable.
