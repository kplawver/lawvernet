Oh yes, you had to know it was coming. Want to run JSP's or other cool Apache add-ons on your swanky [OS X](http://www.apple.com/macosx/) machine? Oh, you say it's a pain in the butt? Yes, it **was**. Now you can go get the [Complete Apache2](http://www.serverlogistics.com/apache2.php "aaronfaby.com - Complete Apache2") from serverlogistics.com and throw those worries out the window. They have packages for PHP, mod\_perl and JSP. Now, I'm not terribly familiar with Apache, but I've loved having it on my Powerbook as a platform for playing with [Movable Type](http://www.movabletype.org). Being able to run a servlet engine on it makes OS X just about perfect, don't you think?
