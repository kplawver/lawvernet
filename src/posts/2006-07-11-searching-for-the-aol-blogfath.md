For a while, I thought that I might have been the first AOL employee to have a blog. I know that can't be true, but I'm curious as to who the AOL Blogfather is (term stolen gracelessly from [Reid](http://photodude.com), who is one of my blogfathers).

If you're an AOL or Netscape current or former employee and you have a blog whose first post is older than [7/20/2000](/archive/2000/07/20/h16_welcome_to_lawvernet.php), please let me know (you know, post a comment). It would be nice if the blog was still active.

Why do this today? Well, another great AOLer has started a blog, [Jacob Rosenberg](http://www.jacobrosenberg.net/). Jacob's a great guy and one of my favorite operations folks. His second post is a short history of the [many web servers that have come out of AOL](http://www.jacobrosenberg.net/2006/07/11/the-many-web-servers-of-aol/). I've used all of them at some point, and remember them (mostly) fondly.

Only nine days until this blog's sixth birthday. I still haven't decided what to get it yet.
