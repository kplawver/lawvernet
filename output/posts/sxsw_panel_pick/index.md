The SXSW Panel Picker is finally up, and y'all can go vote for our panel proposal! It's called [Career Transitions: From DIY to Working for the Man](http://panelpicker.sxsw.com/ideas/view/157) and will be a discussion about moving between different jobs, and coping mechanisms for making those transitions - from big company to small, to startup, to government and anywhere in between. If it gets accepted, you'll get to see [Jason Garber](http://sixtwothree.org), [Cindy Li](http://cindyli.com), [Thomas Vander Wal](http://vanderwal.net) and me live and in person... which will be a _treat_.\\ [Go vote now or Jesus will be sad!](http://panelpicker.sxsw.com/ideas/view/157)
