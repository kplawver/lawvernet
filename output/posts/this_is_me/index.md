Just substitute chocolate for the wine: [Come home, Daddy!](http://www.amalah.com/amalah/2007/08/the-suicide-hou.html)\\ I can handle the kids the first 90% of the time Kevin is gone. Once that anticipation of Kevin coming home hits me, I am done for. If he is just at work, then I start to lose it around 4 pm. If he is gone on a three-day business trip, I lose it about half way through the third day. If he is gone for three weeks, I am fine for 18 days in a row- IN A ROW!!- until I sense that he will soon be home. It's weird to me that I can be fine for 18 days in a row but can't always handle a whole regular work day. I guess it is because I know I have to handle it when he is away on business.
