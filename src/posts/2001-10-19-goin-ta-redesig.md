**Goin' ta redesign** - Yes, yes I am. I want something lighter and a little different. I've been playing with the idea of using Fisher Price little people in some way, I just haven't figured out how. My mom brought out all my old toys for [Max](/Max), and one of his favorites is my old Little People house and its inhabitants. They include the bald green guy, Donna Reed, a butler in a tux and tophat, two little kids, an Indian chief and a construction worker (both part of that classic Little People collection, the Village People Backstage Baccanal Playset). Stay tuned... it should get interesting.
