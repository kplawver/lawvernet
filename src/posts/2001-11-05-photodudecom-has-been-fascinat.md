[PhotoDude.com](http://www.photodude.com/index.shtml) has been fascinating to read over the past few months. I used to go for the photos. They're beautiful. Since 9/11, though, he's turned his blog into a totally different animal. I don't know quite how to explain the change, because I don't **really** know him, but it seems that where there were once soft edges and humor, is all hard corners and harsh words. Interesting.
