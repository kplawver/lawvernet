It's been, what, two days, and you already want to return it?

It's funny to me that as soon as the public outcry against Indiana's "Religious Freedom Restoration Act" involved actual dollars, everyone who fought for it, and who fought every attempt to add protections to it, are holding up their hands and saying, "But, but, we never meant to discriminate against anyone!"

Nonsense. They knew. It was the entire point of the law, otherwise there would be no reason to leave out protections against discrimination by corporations (which is really why this one is awful - if businesses are people, then businesses can have religious beliefs, and can be just as hypocritical as actual people - and don't say everyone else has them - they don't have RFRA's where _businesses_ are included - Indiana's is _special_).

So, here we are with everyone from the governor of Indiana to even the Family Research Council, trying to "clarify" their intentions.

I'm not buying it. If they actually had religious principles, and not just hate, they'd be fighting for the bill as they intended it - as a way to exclude people they don't like from society, and not running away like cowards.

Mr. Governor and friends, faith isn't faith until it's tested. Yours just got tested, and you failed. As soon as the light of public scrutiny fell on you and revealed your hate for what it is, you wilted. You are nothing but faithless hateful cowards.
