date: 2005-02-02

We wanted football jerseys. In the tourist shops and sporting goods stores, they're crazy expensive, so we'd given up hope. Then, we found O'Neills Factory Store, and we were saved!!! It's a factory outlet on Long Mile Road in Dublin, and we got crazy deals on jerseys, hats and even a soccer ball that has **St Kevin's Boys** printed on it.\\ So, don't get suckered in by the tourist places, hit the outlet. You won't be sorry.
