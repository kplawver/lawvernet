I just saw the e-mail today that the book I helped write is going to press on Monday!! It's called [**Adapting to Web Standards: CSS and Ajax for Big Sites**](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0321501829?ie=UTF8&tag=ultranormal-20&linkCode=as2&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=0321501829)![](/assets/images/posts/adapting_to_web/ir), and I wrote a chapter about [AOL.com](http://aol.com). It's available for pre-order now, of course, and would make a _great_ Christmas gift for your favorite web nerd - they might even like two or three copies (really, they might).

Writing the book (well, my chapter) was... difficult. I don't think I'll write another one any time soon, at least while I have a full-time job. It was a great experience, don't get me wrong, and [Christopher](http://www.christopherschmitt.com/) and Victor, our editor, handled all the hard bits. I'd always wanted to write a book, and am grateful that they gave me the opportunity to write a chapter for this one.

I can't wait to hold a copy in my grubby mitts and hear from folks what they think of it (good or bad).
