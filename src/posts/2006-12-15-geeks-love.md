It's scary how easily these things get started. All it took was [one little verbal slip in a meeting](http://lawver.net/archive/2006/10/26/h13_unexpected_truths.php), and now [we have t-shirts](http://www.cindyli.com/index.php/site/comments/unexpected_truths_take_ii/). I got mine today when I made a little trip to the office to show off a prototype. They turned out way better than I expected (because Cindy never sleeps, apparently).

I love having talented friends who like the stupid stuff as much as I do. Work would be really boring without them.
