Jen and I have talked about this one a couple times, and man, I wish [TiVo](http://www.tivo.com) would just do it. TiVo should have playlists. For example, say you have a two year old who loves **Blue's Clues** and you're exhausted because said toddler woke up too early. Wouldn't it be great to hit the **Keep Max Happy** playlist, put it on repeat, and escape to the couch for more sleep? Yeah, that's what I thought.
