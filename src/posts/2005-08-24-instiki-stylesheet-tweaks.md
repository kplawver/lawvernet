As you all know, I love Instiki. I saw a page on the site this morning for [Stylesheet Tweaks](http://instiki.org/show/StylesheetTweaks) and just had to share mine. I don't like how narrow Instiki is by default, and I didn't really like the font. So, I changed it. I've hacked a lot of the templates (to make the textarea larger, for example), but my stylesheet is still mostly default with some significant changes.

Your mileage may vary, especially on the edit page (where I did a good deal of hacking).
