[The Redskins have a new coach](http://sportsillustrated.cnn.com/football/news/2002/01/14/redskins_spurrier_ap/). I'm a little surprised it all happened this quickly, but I guess I shouldn't be. The short owner isn't known for acting with deliberation. Now they have a coach who will at least make them entertaining. I'll be interested to see who they get as GM. That's the real problem. Tony Banks won't work as the QB in Spurrier's system, and well, I doubt anyone else on the roster right now will either. That means they need a GM who can get a top-flight QB without mortgaging too much of the future. Good luck.
