Like I said the other day, [ficlets is up for two SxSW Web Awards](/archive/2008/02/08/h22_the_fics_keep_on_coming.php) this year. Now, part of being a finalist, is that it's _also_ eligible for the [People's Choice Award](http://sxsw.com/peoples_choice/). One of the finalists will win the award based on voting by the public.

Now, it's a long shot for [ficlets](http://ficlets.com) to win. It's up against some very well-funded and popular sites. But, there's a chance. I've already [posted about it on the ficlets blog](http://ficlets.com/blog/entry/vote_early_vote_often) and now I'm posting it here. So... please please please [go vote](http://sxsw.com/peoples_choice/)! You can vote once a day, _every day_ until March 3rd. Please?
