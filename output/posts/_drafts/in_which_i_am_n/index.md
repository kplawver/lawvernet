Warning: there will be much swearing ahead. It's all words you've heard before, but proceed at your own risk.\\ Max's school is having a mini-election between McCain and Obama. Due to his current social studies assignment, we've been talking a lot about politics and history. Kevin and I have explained why we are opposed to Bush and McCain, but I told Max that he could vote for whomever he chooses, that he didn't have to vote the same way we were. That pretty much is the end of me being calm and rational about the upcoming election though.\\ I can sort of understand people voting for McCain, like if there is a single issue so overwhelmingly important that it overrides everything else. But mostly, I can't understand it. I know that McCain isn't Bush, but McCain is totally Bush's lackey. A vote for McCain is a vote that everything Bush did- Iraq, the Katrina aftermath, the Justice Dept firings, the contracts in Iraq, etc, etc, ETC- is all ok. AND IT IS FUCKING NOT OK!! Driving around my neighborhood, I see the occasional McCain yard sign, and I so badly want to egg the house.\\ I am vehemently against Prop 8 on the California ballot and am disheartened by the polls showing that about half are in support of it. What the hell, world? I went to the pro-prop 8 website and listened to why some people are in favor of it. One of the strongest reasons they are touting is because second-graders were read a story about a prince marrying another prince. Like, really, WHAT THE FUCK IS THE BIG DEAL? Don't you want your kids to LIVE tolerance, even if their personal beliefs differ from another's? I have been teaching my kids that some people choose to live with someone of the same sex since the time they could talk. I in no way or shape think this will lead either of them to be gay. BECAUSE IT ISN'T A CHOICE. Seriously, did you choose your sexuality?\\ So some people don't want their kids to learn about homosexuality, or however they word it, at school. Fine. I can be down with that. Just because something is, or will hopefully be, a right (aka gay marriage, gun ownership, obtaining a divorce, using birth control, obtaining an abortion, drinking alcohol\*, attending a certain church, etc) doesn't mean it needs to be explained in depth in school. USE YOUR MOTHERFUCKING BRAINS, PEOPLE.\\ I am so fucking furious that the LDS Church is supporting Prop 8.\\ \*This example brought to you by the Mormon in me. Drinking is bad, y'all!
