There's an excellent article on [Mozilla](http://www.mozilla.org) over at [Salon](http://www.salon.com/tech/col/leon/2002/03/12/mozilla/index.html). As a fanatical Mozilla user since the earliest preview releases (I didn't start using is as my default browser until it got stable enough around .9.2), I have to say that I wouldn't suggest using anything else. I love the e-mail client, the bookmarking system (although there were still some annoying little bugs as of .9.8), and the standards support. .9.9 is out now, and I'd suggest trying it out. Mozilla is a lot faster than it was six months ago and a lot more stable than it was just two months ago. Try it, you'll like it.
