Whenever I wonder why I don't have a PDA, I try to think of [this](http://www.handspring.com/products/communicators/). **That's** what a handheld should do. And yes, I don't have a cell either. I just have my lovely work-provided two-way pager. I don't want an electronic calendar. I want a phone, browser and organizer all in one. Soon, it will be possible, and then I'll think about getting one. I've held out this long, I can wait. That's what I keep telling myself anyway.
