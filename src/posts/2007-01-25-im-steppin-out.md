My pals over at [dev.aol.com](http://dev.aol.com) asked me during [Mashup Camp](http://mashupcamp.com) to start a blog over on [their site](http://dev.aol.com). I've wanted to start a geekier blog to do more long form posts on all things web, but just haven't had the time or drive to really get it set up (you know, I can't use default templates, it's just not me). Well, here's my chance, so I said yes (you know, I don't think I've ever said no to [Naveed](http://flickr.com/photos/kplawver/361211813/in/set-72157594485946520/) - must ponder that one). I posted my [first entry](http://dev.aol.com/node/175) tonight. My goal is to post over there at least once a week - though two a week would be great. I've got way too much going on right now (product managing and writing a lot of the code for a new product, trying to write 1/3 of a book, taking more pictures, preparing for two sessions at [SxSW](http//2007.sxsw.com/interactive), a couple other possible conferences, etc), but I think this is a good thing to support and to help out with. So, check it out if you want, and let me know what you think - suggestions for stuff you'd like me to write about would be greatly appreciated.
