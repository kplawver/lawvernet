I just spent 45 minutes or so watching Designed to Sell's top 35 secrets. I took notes. So, I am now passing them on to you:\\ 35. De-clutter.\\ 34. Clean your house, redo grungy grout if needed.\\ 33. Don't have deferred maintenance. Minor things like crack in the sidewalk should be fixed.\\ 32. Go neutral with paint.\\ 31. Get rid of odors in house: open windows, boil cinnamon, bake cookies or bread, add a scent to the paint at the hardware store.\\ 30. Spring is a great time to put house on the market. December is the worst.\\ 29. Have clean and tody landscaping, add colors with plants and flowers, red mulch in winter.\\ 28. Play up outside architectural features.\\ 27. Focus the front door- make it look new.\\ 26. Tend to the backyard and make it usable space, dress it up like an outdoor room.\\ 25. Stand at the threshold of your house and judge what you see.
