I haven't listened to _any_ of these yet, but here's the list of albums I gleaned from today's new releases on [Rdio](http://rdio.com). I'll try to come back and update them once I've actually listened to them, but I'm a busy guy, so I'll probably forget. Here's the queue as it stands right now:

- [Fleet Foxes - Helplessness Blues](http://rd.io/x/QE9kPkhsQg) - This one's also available for [

$3.99 on Amazon today](http://amzn.to/iWKhvP) so I already bought it. These guys are unapologetically old school folk and _awesome_.
- [Beastie Boys - Hot Sauce Committee Part Two](http://rd.io/x/QE9kPkmDBg)
- [Moment Bends - Architecture in Helsinki](http://rd.io/x/QE9kPkgZfQ) - I have no idea if this is going to be any good as I only vaguely recognize the name of the band, but hey, why not.
- [Portugal The Man - Devil Say I, I Say AIR EP](http://rd.io/x/QE9kPkgZfQ) - I keep hearing about them in the same company with bands I like, so I'm checking out the new stuff.
- [Gingerpig - The Ways of the Gingerpig](http://rd.io/x/QE9kPklrWg) - I just like the name "Gingerpig"
- [Country Mice - Twister](http://rd.io/x/QE9kPkmhqw) - I _think_ this band released a two song single about a month ago and I loved it. I'm hoping this is the full album.
- [Plain White T's - All That We Needed](http://rd.io/x/QE9kPkgOXA) - A band I feel like I should know but don't.
- [Galactic - The Other Side of Midnight, Live in New Orleans](http://rd.io/x/QE9kPkhsaA) - You've seen them on **Treme**, you may have even heard their [awesome live show with Corey Glover from nyctaper](http://www.nyctaper.com/?p=3244), hell, you may have even heard an album of theirs. _Listen to them and you will be happy, dammit!_
    
    - **Update**: This is the first thing I listened to this morning and it's audible caffeine. So so awesome.
- [Antony and the Johnsons - Swanlights EP](http://rd.io/x/QE9kPkhqKA) - I love this guy's songwriting, but I don't often actually like the songs, which sucks. It's a band I respect more than I like, but I keep sampling every new album hoping it's the one that makes it all click.

There are some other things in the queue, but I have to go hop in the shower and get to work. I'll try to hop back in and add other new stuff I find worth listening to and update the guesses with the results. Happy new music tuesday!
