<p>Something that’s bubbled up a few times with clients lately: you can absolutely apply for and interview for a job you have no plans to take, in order to learn something about a company or field. You aren’t wasting their time any more than they are wasting yours if they choose someone else; and they will have a chance to make their case to you if they really want you. Think of applications and interviews as _research_ and act accordingly.</p>
