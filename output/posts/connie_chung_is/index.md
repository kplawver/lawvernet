[Connie Chung is joining CNN](http://www.cnn.com/2002/SHOWBIZ/TV/01/23/connie.chung.cnn/index.html), and will host an hour-long show at 8pm in the Spring. The show hasn't been named yet. In an attempt to be as helpful as possible to the newest member of the AOL-Time Warner family, I've come up with a list of possible titles for her show:

- **On The Bottom Rung With Chung**
    
- She could get together with Wayne Brady from Who's Line and have a Crossfire-like: **Wayne-Chung Hour** (sorry, I really couldn't help it)
    
- In the same vein, she and Larry King could combine their shows for the canned **Chung King Show** (does anyone else remember Chung King canned Chinese food?)
    
- In the spirit of ESPN's new design treatment, they could call it just **CONNIE**
    
- **Pro and Connie**
    
- **On The Down Low with cChung**
    
- She could get together with **A**aron Brown and Wolf **B**litzer and call it **The News, Brought to You By A, B and C**
