[David Byrne](http://www.luakabop.com/david_byrne/index.html)'s **Look Into The Eyeball** is grrrrrrrrrrrr-eat. I got it from Jen's parents and I can't stop listening to it. Every song is perfectly layered. If I turn up the volume, there are small nuances that I didn't hear before, and every listen exposes some new inflection or chord I didn't hear before. Mr. Byrne, you are my hero.

The [Six Feet Under](http://www.hbo.com/sixfeetunder/) is very nice too, as is **Bricolage**, by Amon Tobin. I even got Lyle Lovett's first album and a great little blue's compilation. Hooray for music!
