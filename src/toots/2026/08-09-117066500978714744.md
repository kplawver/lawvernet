<p>I watched Dug Dug this morning. It’s an Indian movie that feels like a fairy tale told by David Lynch (more Straight Story than Fire Walk With Me). It’s meandering, charming, funny and really beautiful to look at most of the time, especially the beginning. </p><p>Definitely worth watching, even if it’s just to get lost in something trying to figure out what the hell is going on. Also, the less you know going in the better.</p>
