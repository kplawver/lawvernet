[Part One](http://www.lancearthur.com/archives/000017.html) and [Part Two](http://www.lancearthur.com/archives/000004.html). I sure hope Mr. Arthur sounds like Buddy Cole, because he does in my head. I have never read a funnier parody of high school assemblies, the differences between straight and gay, and classroom discussions. He did a great job capturing voice and making it feel like it was an actual transcript. Funny, funny stuff.\\ Aside from the humor, I was impressed by how honest it is about the camps in the gay community. In Tucson, I knew a lot of gay people and had quite a few gay friends. It was funny to see parties and gatherings where the group was mostly gay. The cliques and stereotypes are mostly true in a beautiful and amusing way. Lance does a better job than I could of putting all those stereotypes in perspective and doing it with a lot of humor.\\ And relatedly... I miss all my HomoFriends. No one that I know here is out enough to be any fun. Maybe I'm just old and have no friends... Maybe the gay people all run from me and my obvious boringness. No, really, I can queen out if you give me a chance! I'm straight, but that doesn't mean we can't be friends!!
