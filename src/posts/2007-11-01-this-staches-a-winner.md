[![Kevin, showing off two months of 'stache-growing.](/assets/images/posts/this_staches_a_winner/1809448001_ca6e42e384.jpg)](http://flickr.com/photos/kplawver/1809448001/in/set-72157601799509224/)

They [posted the final tally](http://thegreatstacheoff.com/current-rankings/) and **I won**! I have no idea what the trophy looks like or when it will show up, but expect pictures when it gets here.

There's no way I would have won if not for [Howard Uman](http://twitter.com/unhuman), who sent an e-mail out to about 800 people (no, really, 750 people inside AOL, his hockey team and who knows who else) asking them to donate. I felt weird about hitting people up at work for money, so Howard did it for me. Expect a picture of Howie's smiling face holding the trophy in the near future. Thank you, Howard!!

It was harder than I thought it would be to keep a mustache for two months. It [looked ridiculous](http://flickr.com/photos/kplawver/sets/72157601799509224/), itched a lot, was hard to trim (I was always afraid I'd make it uneven, so I just didn't trim it until I was taking bites of it with my food)... I mean, who wants to look like the fat extra on **CHiPS**?

But, now it's over. We raised some money to save some nuts (I guess I can stop with the testicle jokes now), and had some fun in the process. Thank you to all 29 people who donated, the [guys who organized it](http://thegreatstacheoff.com/), Jen for putting up with the 'stache and not making _too_ many mustache ride jokes, and everyone else for playing along and not giggling too much when I entered the room.
