**The Delgados'** **Everybody Come Down** makes me think of little kids running around, uncontrollable in their joy and energy. It builds tempo, has those great jangling guitars, and relentless beat. It reminds me of [Max](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kplawver/2450776/) when he gets beaned up (or [Tim](http://tim.lawver.net)).

It also sounds a little like **The Breeders** **Cannonball**, but better and more fun.

The rest of the album (**Universal Audio**) is great too. Very well put together pop rock, with some really punchy lyrics.
