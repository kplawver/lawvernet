In my recent techno-obsession, I've forgotten all about Phish. Today, I'm listening to a bunch of their albums, and the first one I picked up is still my favorite. [Billy Breathes](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/tg/detail/-/B000002HNP/) is an amazing album. In the album's 45 minutes, Phish manages to use all of their talent to create an album that sounds better than anything else they've done. It has all the punch, joy and great riffs of their other stuff without any of the self-indulgence. This is a great album if you want to try Phish on for size before plowing into their weirder stuff (or live albums).
