I've been a Netflix customer for only a few months, but I'm already in love with all the foreign films and documentaries I can get. The best part, though, has to be all the British TV shows! Because someone asked on Twitter last night, here's a list of my favorite British stuff on Netflix right now:

- [Luther](http://movies.netflix.com/WiMovie/Luther/70175633?trkid=2361637) - One of the best psychological thrillers and cop shows I've seen in a long time. Idris Elba is fantastic as John Luther, and the rest of the cast is superb. I can't recommend this one highly enough. Fantastic show.
- [Doctor Who](http://movies.netflix.com/WiMovie/Doctor_Who/70142441?trkid=2361637) - If you've missed the Doctor Who revival of the last six years, what's wrong with you?! This show is _aces_. It walks a fine line between serious sci-fi, camp, satire and horror. The great thing about Doctor Who is that it can do pretty much every genre and still be OK for kids (I tell mine that the Doctor always wins and that seems to get them through all the scary stuff).
- [Top Gear](http://movies.netflix.com/WiPlayer?movieid=70140457&trkid=4447188&t=Top+Gear+%28U.K.%29) - Again, how have you missed this? Don't be afraid that it's a car show - it's basically the Three Stooges with insanely expensive toys. It's one of the funniest shows ever produced, and there's a reason it's one of the most popular shows _in the world_.
- [The IT Crowd](http://movies.netflix.com/WiMovie/The_IT_Crowd/70140450?trkid=2361637) - One of the funniest sitcoms ever, and one of the geekiest. Really, just watch it.
- [Downton Abbey](http://movies.netflix.com/WiMovie/Masterpiece_Classic_Downton_Abbey/70155164?trkid=2361637) - Kind of an **Upstairs Downstairs** thing, and a little soapy in spots, but the acting and writing are spectacular, especially the servants.
- [That Mitchell and Webb Look](http://movies.netflix.com/WiMovie/That_Mitchell_and_Webb_Look/70142357?trkid=2361637) - Seriously funny sketch show - smart too.

I've got to get to work now, but that should keep you busy. I know I've missed a bunch, and some of my favorite UK shows aren't on yet (**Misfits** is one of my favorites - think **Skins** with super powers), but keep an eye out.

I'd love to hear about new stuff if there are things I'm missing!
