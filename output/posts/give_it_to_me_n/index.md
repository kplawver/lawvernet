I know I've been slacking about posting, but I have perfectly valid reasons. Like anything with life, stuff runs hot and cold. Work is hot right now. Blogging - eh - not so hot. I'll still post daily, but probably not the deluge I had been offering. Also, life is hot. Max has decided that sleeping is not his 'thing' at the moment, and that means everyone at the Lawver house is doing a little adjusting. Jen's not getting her normal nap because Max isn't taking his. We're staying up later because Max is. We're getting up earlier, cuz, yet again, Max is. So, we're making due with Carry-Out and lots of help from my family (and moral support from her parents in Tucson). Once I wake up, I'm sure I'll be all chatty and sharing again.

In the sharing vein (which, kids, you should never do. Remember, sharing veins is for foreigners and weirdos. If you share veins, the terrorists have already won! Ok, you can ignore me now.), I've rediscovered [iTunes](http://www.apple.com/itunes) radio tuner feature, and am totally addicted to [Groove Salad](http://www.somafm.com/ "SomaFM (scroll down)"). It's trancey, groovy and conducive to half-awake coding.

In good work news, my new LogCrunching machine should be here in the next week or so. It will be berry berry nice:

- Dual Xeon 1.7ghz
    
- 1gb Rambus RAM
    
- 2 36gb SCSI hard drive
    
- nVidia Quadro2 video card w/ 64 mb
    
- dual NICS
    
- RedHat 7.2
    
- Sweet free mouse pad
    

What will I be doing with this machine? I'll be crunching lots of files and extracting numbers from them like a [SuperJuicer](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/B00005QFL1/ref%3Dpd%5Fnfy%5Fnr%5Fk%5F13/102-3691778-1249764). How? I can't post the code or anything, but I'll be using [AOLserver](http://www.aolserver.com) because it does a great job of scheduling jobs and I already know it. I'll use [Postgres](http://www.postgresql.org) to dump the numbers into. It will be lots and lots of fun and give me my desktop machine back so I don't have to run the numbers I run now on it. A winner all around.
