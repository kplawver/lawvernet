Sometimes it is weird having a blog (and twitters) with my spouse. Usually I really like it, especially when Kev writes about activities that we've done together. Today, though, is one of the odd days because he twittered that he woke up in a bad mood. I didn't know this. He seemed perfectly chipper this morning. Every time he passes by me I make him stop so I can kiss and hug on him. He seemed receptive. Had I known he was in a bad mood I would have left him alone, cuz no one wants to be petted and bothered while brooding. Now that I know, I feel like I have to retroactively not bother him and walk on eggshells.
