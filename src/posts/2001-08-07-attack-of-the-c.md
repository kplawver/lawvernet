[Attack of the Clones](http://www.cnn.com/2001/SHOWBIZ/Movies/08/07/star.wars.2/index.html) Huh? Ok, so, the name of Star Wars Episode 2 is going to be **Star Wars: Attack of the Clones**. It's apparently about the Clone Wars mentioned way back in **Star Wars** (you know, the one that came out first). Why not just call it **Star Wars: The Clone Wars**? Other than repetetive, at least it doesn't sound like a bad Ed Wood movie, starring Jesse Helms and Trent Lott as the intrepid CloneBusters. Lucas has gone soft in the head, I swear.
