My little brother asked me this week what it would take to set up a blog. He played right into my trap. It took twenty minutes to set up the new domain, MySQL db, an hour to wait while [Dreamhost](http://www.dreamhost.com) got them all set up, then another 10 minutes to install and configure [Textpattern](http://www.textpattern.com). I'm still getting used to Textpattern (the look is all my fault), so look for changes, and maybe a real domain name in the future. With all that said, I proudly present, my little brother, compatriot, super pal and best friend: [Tim](http://tim.lawver.net/ "Tim's Weblog") (the mighty).
