Even though my kids have been sick before, I feel like I am earning my 'mommy stripes' for the first time. Brian doesn't take his medicine from me easily and he hurts whenever he moves even a little bit. I have never been so thankful that he is still wearing pull-ups! He's camped out on the couch in the basement since there is a tv down there, but he never seems remotely comfortable. His head and upper body keeps sliding towards the edge of the couch so we have to move him back every so often. This leads to him yelling, "Don't hurt me!" Ahh, my heart. I slept in the basement last night too and at one point I woke up to him hanging mostly off of the couch and headed toward the floor. (Earlier I put a large cushion down there in case he did fall off of the couch, but I can't imagine the cushion would offset the arm pain.) It's definitely not an ideal situation. Should he be in this much pain in between the doses of his pain medicine?\\ Yesterday he only had some juice, water, 2 rice cakes, and 2 cookies. That can't be good for him either, but I guess we can't really deal with a bowel movement right now.\\ Max has been staying at his grandma's house since before I took Brian to the doctor. She was also kind enough to make us a turkey dinner. Yum.
