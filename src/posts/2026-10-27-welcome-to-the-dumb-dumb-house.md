Inspired by Ethan Marcotte's [meme collection](https://bukk.it) a _long_ time ago, I too created a directory and threw my memes in it. It was _also_ just an Apache directory listing. And then, one day, my hosting provider decided that I couldn't have fun and disabled Apache directory listings.  Was I deterred?  NO!

I half-assedly built a site in Hugo to host all my memes, and there it sat, half done and unloved for many years until I got inspired by something we did at work where we did an experiment to see how quickly different AI models could build blogs with 11ty.  For the Haiku run, I told it to create a "childish garish blog called 'Welcome to the Dumb Dumb House'."  The design was so stupid, I fell in love.

So, this weekend, inspired by the _spirit_ of The Dumb Dumb House, I recreated my meme repository as a living breathing [Dumb Dumb House](https://lols.lawver.net). It's so stupid, and not done, but I think it's so ridiculous, I will keep playing with it, and add more images and see just how silly I can make it.  

Because that's what the web _should_ be. It should be a field full of freak flags.  Yours shouldn't be _trapped_ in the algorithmic strangle of Facebook, Instagram or, god forbid, Twitter.  It should be on _your own site_ where you control what's displayed first and it can be as _freaky_ as you want it to be.
