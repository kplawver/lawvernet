Every year, [SxSW](http://sxsw.com) posts a _ton_ of free mp3's from the artists that showcase at the music festival. And every year since 2004, I've [downloaded](http://sites.google.com/site/sxsw2009torrent/) _all_ of them. This year, though, instead of just letting them take up space in iTunes, I'm going to try something. Between now and whenever I get through them, I'm going to try to listen to a letter a day (the playlist is alphabetical by artist) and do my best to rate every song. Then, I'm going to post my top ten songs from each letter here. Here are the A's:

1. [**Gardenias and Cigarettes** by _Ana Silvera_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=84199) - 5 stars - I love this song. It's a classic gin-drenched torch song.
2. [**The River** by _Anathallo_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=91193) - 5 stars - Another great ballad. If Bloc Party were acoustic piano rock, they'd sound like this.
3. [**The Thief & The Heartbreaker** by _Alberta Cross_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=83576) - 4 stars
4. [**Si Pero No** by _Alex Cuba_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=91709) - 4 stars
5. [**Amanha eu nao vou trabalhar** by _Alexandre Grooves_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=75975) - 4 stars
6. [**Government Meat** by Ali Eskandarian](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=79082) - 4 stars
7. [**To All Tiny Creatures** by _All Tiny Creatures_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=93663) - 4 stars
8. [**Leeds United** by _Amanda Palmer_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=92813) - 4 stars
9. [**One More Day** by _Amy Wadge_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=84632) - 4 stars
10. [**Set The Bears Free** by _Anchorsong_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=81927) - 4 stars
11. [**Oh, The Boss Is Coming** by _Arkells_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=88064) - 4 stars - I have to say, these guys sound a _lot_ like The Constantines, which may be why I dug this song so much.
12. [**These Flowers of Ours** by _The Asteroid #4_](http://sxsw.com/music/shows/schedule/?a=show&s=85237) - 4 stars - Reminds me a lot of The Dandy Warhols.\\ This is totally unscientific, and I probably missed rating some songs (I do need to work during the day), but here are the A's. I'm listening to the B's today. And yes, I know there are more than ten, but they're alphabetical within the number of stars, so I decided to list all the 4 and 5 star songs.
