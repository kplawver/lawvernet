I love it when I can pick out the songs used in commercials. You know that new Putnam commercial with the picture frame flying around the office? The commercial's fairly forgettable, but the music isn't. It's from [Lemon Jelly](http://www.lemonjelly.ky/html/index.html)'s great **In The Bath**, which is on their peachy-keen **Lemonjelly.ky**.

I have two of their albums, the one mentioned above, and **Lost Horizons**. I can't recommend them highly enough. They're loopy, happy, goofy fun. Definitely check them out.
