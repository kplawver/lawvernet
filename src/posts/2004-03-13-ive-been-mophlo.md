And apparently, I'm one of two [monsters of blog ...](http://sxsw.blogs.com/moblog/2004/03/monsters_of_blo.html "Moblog: monsters of blog ..."). The picture was taken by [George](http://www.allaboutgeorge.com), and the guy standing next to me is Chuck Olsen, who's making a [blogumentary](http://www.blogumentary.org). Oh, and we were all at Break Bread With Brad, which was great. I met a bunch of bloggers I've been reading for years, some I've heard of, and others who were all new to me. Everyone was super-swell, and I had a great time.

After breaking bread (which I didn't... I don't think I ever sat down for more than five minutes), we all went to Polly Esther's for Karaoke. Watching [Smokler](http://www.kevinsmokler.com/) cover **U2** will be burned into my retinas and eardrums for days.

Big thanks to [Michael](http://www.chatterwaul.com/odd) and Ari for introducing me to folks, and for being great ambassadors for their city. You guys rule.

Oh, and just so Jen doesn't worry, I did eat dinner. I got two pieces of pizza at **Onion** on 5th Street at 11:30 as I walked back to the hotel.
