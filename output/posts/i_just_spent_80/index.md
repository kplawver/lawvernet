More from me-\\ I get that the LDS church wants to silence dissents, but this guy isn't speaking out about church doctrine. The petition states: **We affirm the LDS Church's right to define doctrine and policy for its own members.** So, let the church proclaim all it wants about whatever. Let the church dictate the actions of its members, bestowing religious privileges only upon whom they see fit. But when it comes to denying _civil rights_ to others? I just can't support that all. Doctrine and Covenants 134:9 states, "We do not believe it just to mingle religious influence with civil government..." So, yea. There ya go.\\ See the petition here: [Signing for Something](http://signingforsomething.org/blog/?page_id=267).
