This is my fifth year at SxSW. Something wonderful happens to me when I'm here with all my crazy creative friends. Some switch gets flip and the tenuous control I have over what comes out of my mouth completely switches off and I become a round mound of manic energy. The pictures have already started showing up on flickr (thanks to [MD](http://m-dnovember.com) for documenting it):

At **Break Bread With Brad** last night, I won a lovely custom made purse by [Cinnamon Cooper](http://poise.cc). Now, I'm not normally a purse _guy_, but I made an exception last night and carried it as we wandered around Austin. Jen, you're getting a really cute little handbag when I get home...

[![Kevin smiling and holding a tiny handbag](/assets/images/posts/something_in_th/2318389600_9ae9024650.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/m-d/2318389600/)

And then there was [Dave](http://daviddylanthomas.com). I mentioned that Dave had lost a _lot_ of weight since last year, and asked him, of course, if it was a tapeworm. Unfortunately, it was really a medical condition. Thankfully, Dave's a funny guy, and now "Hey, that tapeworm is stealing my food!" became the first running gag at SxSW for us this year. I don't know that the picture really captures it, but that's what we were going for.

[![Dave Thomas is stealing my marshmallow!](/assets/images/posts/something_in_th/2318390878_e7c4541537.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/m-d/2318390878/)

Today, it's off to some panels, and tomorrow, the Web Awards and [Fray](http://fray.com). I love SxSW.
