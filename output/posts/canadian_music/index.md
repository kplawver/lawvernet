I've decided that today is my personal **Canadian Music Day**. I started this morning with the [CBC Radio 3 Podcast](http://www.cbcradio3.com/podcast), and have now moved on to **The Arcade Fire**. I'll probably finish up the day with **The Constantines** and [James'](http://consolationchamps.com) mix cd from SxSW '05 (not all Canadian music, but since it was compiled by a Canadian, I'm declaring it OK).\\ **Update**: Thanks to James' aforementioned mix cd, I've found some more music to fill Canadian Music Day. This afternoon will be composed of (none of which I've listened to yet):

- **Rheostatics** - **Night of the Shooting Stars**
- **The Magnetic Fields** - **i** (oops, they're not Canadian, but James put them on the CD!)
- **Martin Tielli** - **We Didn't Even Suspect He Was the Poppy Salesman**
- **The New Pornographers** - **Twin Cinema**\\ Yes, record companies, this is what happens when you let people play music on the internet - we buy more music. I never would have purchased any of this music if not for the internet, if not for mix cd's. Give away a little, and people will return the favor by buying a lot.
