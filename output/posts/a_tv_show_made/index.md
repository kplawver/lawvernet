I saw a commercial for the tv show, _My Kid Is Smarter Than Me_. This isn't a jokey show like _Are You Smarter Than a 5th Grader?_, it's a reality show about kiddie-geniuses and how their parents try to keep up with them, keep them grounded, and well-rounded. Sounds like something Kevin and I should check out, yes? The show seems to focus on young kids. Parenting Max when he was three was relatively easy. I would make up chemical compounds so Max could compute its atomic mass and he would be in heaven. But now, wooo, boy. A couple of years ago when Kevin came home from work, Max welcomed him with, "Quantum physics is hard!" Imagine what Max's brain is like now!!!!
