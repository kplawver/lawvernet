The [Paid Content article](http://www.paidcontent.org/aol-buddy-lists-social-network-expands-with-aim-pages-phoneline) has a lot more detail about what's coming and says more than I can right now.

Stay tuned for more info. After we launch the real thing, I'll be able to talk a _lot_ more about it.
