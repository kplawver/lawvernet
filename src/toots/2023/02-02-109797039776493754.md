US History courses glossed over it, even in college.</p><p>Once you hear it, the entire history of the American black experience makes sense. Slavery was unspeakably horrific and a segment of the population didn't just survive it, but daily effects continue 100s of years later in every aspect of life.</p><p>This is why they attack African American studies programs.</p>
