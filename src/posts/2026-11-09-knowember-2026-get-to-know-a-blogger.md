It's been a _long time_ since I joined in a blogging game, but I saw this one yesterday and well, I couldn't resist.  Robert Birming, who is apparently really good at coming up with these kinds of things, came up with [Knowember](https://robertbirming.com/knowember/), a change to introduce readers to the person behind the blog.  Now, this blog's been around since 2000, but I can't remember the last time I actually wrote an introduction post other than the [About page](/about).  Maybe never?  Now's as good a time as any!

Ok, here we go...

#### What my blog is mostly about

I thought this would be easy to answer, but I've been staring at it for two minutes and I've got nothing.  

If you look back over the last 3 years of posts, it's mostly me wrestling with my place in the world as a middle aged white guy, which amounts to "old man shakes fist at sky," sprinkled with media recommendations.  Over the last 26 years, though, and I hope, more often than not, it's a decent representation of who I am at the moment I write something down - messy, fairly unfiltered and unedited, as any personal blog should be.  

I _should_ be embarassed to go back ten years and look at any random post, and even more so to go back twenty.  I write about tech, baking, TV, music, and whatever else strikes my fancy.  I'd probably have more readers if I stuck to one thing, but at this point, I don't do this for anyone else but me - also, I think, what personal blogs are about.  

It's my corner fo the web and I'll do what I want with it.
