I'm not sure what to say about the boys' costumes this year, other than, I think I've broken my children. Did they want to be characters from mainstream popular culture? Nope. They wanted to be the main characters from the very funny, but very internet-y [Laugh Out Loud Cats](http://www.hopotopia.com). Did anyone in the neighborhood have the slightest clue who they were? Nope. I don't think that bothered the boys a whole lot, but I do worry that I've done irreparable harm to their pop culture repositories by showing them funny stuff on the internet like [Homestar Runner](http://www.homestarrunner.com) instead of more mainstream fare. I don't know, but they're so damned cute as niche web comic characters, I kind of don't care.
