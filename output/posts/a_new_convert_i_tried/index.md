I tried it before, when it was new. It wasn't ready, and neither was my poor little Powerbook. I couldn't logon to my ISP, watch DVD's or do much of anything. Then, I got my new machine, and the update and now, I am a [OS X](http://www.apple.com/macosx/) convert. I hate to admit it. I got the machine to have a machine to run [YellowDog](http://www.yellowdoglinux.com) on. Now, I spend my time in the bubble gum world of OS X, drinking in the stoplight icons, the warm fuzziness of the first daunting but now comfortable UI. Next thing you know, I'll be using it more than my PC and then I'll become a designer and wear turtlenecks, small glasses and tight jeans. I'll drink small coffees and complain about how those stupid production people ruin my designs.

I admit it, that's a longshot, but I do like OS X, which I NEVER thought would happen. See, Alice, people CAN change.
