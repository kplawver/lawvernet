Once upon a time, in a land far, far away, lived a pauper and his beautiful, funny, brilliant, awesome wife. The pauper happily worked for A Large Company and goofed off with his pot head friends. The opportunity arose to move across the kingdom for an awesome job that made pittance. Thinking that one job is better than two, the couple broke the hearts of friends and family and moved. Slowly, the pauper was able to slay dragons and compile enough gold coins to become a fine knight.\\ Years went by, things were good, poop monsters were birthed.\\ The knight decided he wanted to be a pirate. His wife supported this decision and said she would adopt the 'yo ho ho' lifestyle. But the knight decided to stay with A Large Company instead.\\ Another year went by.\\ The knight was still restless. His was wife was still supportive. The knight still decided to stay with A Large Company.\\ Another one, two, three? years went by.\\ The knight was still restless. His was wife was still supportive. The knight still decided to stay with A Large Company.\\ When the youngest poop monster got into the Company school, the wife said, "You must be a knight for another year."\\ The knight denounced his position, but gave up being a pirate too.\\ He's now a prince.
