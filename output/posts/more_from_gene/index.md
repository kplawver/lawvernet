More from Gene Weingarten. In his latest [Below the Beltway](http://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/articles/A59115-2002Jan3.html) column, Gene updates us on his article about the worst town in America. He found it in Battle Mountain, Nevada. The original article is [here](http://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/articles/A31628-2001Nov28.html). Apparently, the publisher of the town's local paper fired the editor over her jokingly agreeing with Gene about the whole thing. Gene's application to be the new editor is priceless.
