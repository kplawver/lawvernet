I'm a shameful loser for not posting this earlier, but please go check out [Mike Golay's new album](http://www.bansheewerks.com/frivolities/geetar/across_the_bridge/). I haven't heard it yet past some demos during the summer, but if it's anything like his first album, you won't be disappointed. If you like acoustic guitar, you'll love Mike's stuff.

Oh, and you can find his first album in the iTunes Music Store, which was really weird for me. Mike used to be my boss, and finding your boss in iTunes? That's just weird.
