Max received a fish tank for his birthday. Wheee. The boys and I have been having lots of fun watching the fish. He picked out two little tiny ones (per my strongly worded suggestion) but now that the fish are home and I see how big the original fish are, I am worried for the new little guys. Hope they aren't eaten!\\ ETA- I did some googling after posting this entry and the two types of fish aren't compatible. Plus the kind of fish I bought are happier in a school, but I only bought 2 of them. And I learned that goldfish continue to grow so that I shouldn't have gotten more fish anyway. Lastly, we have been feeding the fish the wrong type of food! Oy, I am a bad fish mommy.
