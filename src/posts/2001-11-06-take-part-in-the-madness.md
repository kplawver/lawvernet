Take part in the [madness](http://www.salon.com/mwt/comics/2001/11/06/lynda15/index.html) that is poodle beat poetry (and bonus: [poodle astrology](http://www.salon.com/mwt/comics/barry/2001/08/31/lynda6/index.html)). I love Lynda Barry. It took me the longest time to start reading her comic in the Tucson Weekly. I thought it was sloppy and didn't make any sense. Then, I woke up, and got hooked on Marlys. Now, she's unleashed the poodle, and I can't stop laughing.
