I listen to a lot of podcasts on my commute. The [Heretics](http://thisamericanlife.org/Radio_Episode.aspx?episode=304) episode of **This American Life** hit close to home this week. I've been grappling with my faith now for a while, and there's so much other _stuff_ going on in my life right now that I keep pushing it into the background. But, this episode, about a Pentecostal minister who no longer believes in Hell and how his personal religious transformation affected those around him and his congregation, has been keeping me thinking.\\ I am a heretic now. I've been one in a lot of little ways for a while, but certainly not publicly, and not in any serious way. But now? I'm sure I've crossed the line, and I'm not sure what that means exactly. Unfortunately, right now, I don't have time to figure it all out. But you should go [listen to the show](http://thisamericanlife.org/Radio_Episode.aspx?episode=304). It's a good one.
