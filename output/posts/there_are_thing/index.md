There are things I probably should be doing now, but I can't seem to get started. Motivation, where did you go? It's probably at home singing the ABC song along with [Max](/Max). Instead of being my echo in the four or five songs he knows, he'll now sing along at the top of his lungs. If I slow down the tempo, he slows down. If I go fast, he does his best to keep up. My favorite part is the middle of the alphabet. He enunciates all his letters very well until he gets to el-em-en-oh-poo. I love watching his face as his tongue flails around trying to get the letters out faster. It comes out like el-ah-no-BEEE! And then it's back to normal through to Z. I love my kid, can you tell?
