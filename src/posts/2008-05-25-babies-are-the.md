So, we're watching the end of _Knocked Up_ and Kevin gets giddy and gooey about babies. I say, "Don't go getting any ideas." He is so happy though. I tear up, not wanting to fail him, "Ahh, you're gonna make me cry." \[He thinks I am joking, but my sniffles are real!\] And then he says the best thing ever, "No, we're done. We have ours." OMG, relief. But babies are totally the cutest thing ever and we'll be sure to plaster slobbery kisses all over his brother's baby that's due this summer.
