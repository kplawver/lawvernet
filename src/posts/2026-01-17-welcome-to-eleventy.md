After 25 years on WordPress, the blog has a new home! This site is now powered by [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/), a simpler, faster static site generator.

## Why Eleventy?

- **Speed**: Static HTML files load instantly
- **Security**: No database, no plugins to exploit
- **Simplicity**: Write in Markdown, deploy anywhere
- **Ownership**: All content lives in plain text files
