A couple or three months ago, I gave up reading news sites like CNN and Salon. Boy have I been happier ever since. Yea.\\ I don't feel well and everything hurts and my brain is kind of foggy. So now probably isn't the best time to try to write a post. But I am also tired of just lying around feeling bad. I need something to distract myself. So writing it is.\\ So, where was I? Right, no news. It's been nice. Since my state already had its primary, I don't care what Obama and Hilary are saying to each other. I don't really know what else is going on either. According to The Daily Show (which used clips from various news programs), there isn't a food shortage. Good to know. According to PTI, Roger Clemmons is scum, allegedly. According to The View, Miley Cyrus has some new photos out.\\ My lack of CNN reading means I haven't read about any moms who died tragically when their cars got swept off a bridge but was able to save most of her children. That is definitely a good thing.
