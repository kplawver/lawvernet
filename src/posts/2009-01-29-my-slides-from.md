I finished speaking at \*The Future of Social Media"... it was fun telling them not to join twitter if they're just going to be marketers and not actually be human. I told them other stuff too, I think.

I didn't have a lot of time to talk about the future, so I didn't get to talk about identity vs. persona and my three categories of social networks... maybe next time. I'm pretty sure I scared the hell out of them when I talked about reputation stuff.

Some things I mentioned that either I didn't put the URL for in the slides or didn't have in the slides at all:

- The quote from Jeremy Tanner about twitter spammers comes from [his fantastic blog post - read the whole thing](http://jeremytanner.com/hate-the-player-its-not-a-game/).
- You can read [The Cluetrain Manifesto](http://www.cluetrain.com/book) online for free.
- I talked a little bit about [Seth Godin](http://sethgodin.typepad.com/). His blog may be a little pat, but I've learned a lot about marketing and product development from his books.
- My interest in reputation started with Cory Doctorow's [Down and Out in The Magic Kingdom](http://craphound.com/down/). It introduces the idea of "whuffie" which captured my imagination. I hope to some day implement a real whuffie system online. I came _really_ close once.

I think that's it... hopefully the people who saw it enjoyed it and got something out of it. It was a lot of fun preparing it.
