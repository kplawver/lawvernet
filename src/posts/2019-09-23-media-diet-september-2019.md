I did one of these [earlier in the summer](https://lawver.net/2019/08/summer-media-consumption/), so this one will be a little shorter. I was sick for a while, and we had to evacuate for a hurricane, so I've watched a bunch of stuff.

- **[Bahubali 1 and 2](https://www.netflix.com/title/80204118)**: On Netflix. Two of the highest grossing movies in Indian history. They're both gorgeous, and because I have no background in the underlying mythology, completely insane and inscrutible. Highly recommended just for the sheer spectacle of it.

<iframe src="https://giphy.com/embed/bvXHM2BcAsfzG" allowfullscreen width="480" height="219"></iframe>

- **[The Spy](https://www.netflix.com/title/80178151)**: On Netflix. This kind of developed into a theme the past couple of months. Sasha Baron Cohen plays a spy who infiltrates the Syrian government. Saying more would spoil it, but it's compelling and heartbreaking. The acting throughout is amazing.
- **Our Boys**: On HBO. Another based on a true story, around murder, retaliation, mistrust and politics. Heartbreaking, tense and frustrating. Very worth watching.
- **Operation Finale**: On Prime. About capturing Adolf Eichmann in Argentina. Oscar Isaac and Ben Kingsley are great in it.
- **[Mindhunter Season 2](https://occ-0-3934-27.1.nflxso.net/dnm/api/v6/jsgGe9NCko3c-j0wGZ7FSwVlHs0/AAAABUra7iFPUSNRItJr00n6Gm3BmJYP5W9VT7lhr3eel7xn7x_UUg_pxAX1vYS6RjAFQ87L3t6y09DLm6jZbkx28q-9rNhtJ89oG94gMukNRM-nStgsGIeKx92tXg63rBkBC2khGdTTz4x4Th-_kLrMoUMv55OlAoozxvZqwRjwPtw.png?r=099)**: On Netflix. Another one of those anxiety-ridden shows. I wish they'd left out the bit with the son and spent more time on BTK, but I'll still anxiously await the third season.

That's not everything I watched, but I started several shows and kind of sputtered out on them, or have forgotten them, which might be worse. I am thrilled that **The Great British Bake Off** (cough, sorry) _**Baking Show**_ is back and it's still perfect. I wish there were more competition shows that could feel that fun.
