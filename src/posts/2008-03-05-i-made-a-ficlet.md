I wasn't on the ball this year and didn't get around to ordering more ficlets stickers for SxSW this year. But, since ficlets is up for _two_ SxSW Web Awards this year, I decided I need to do _something_ special. So, I made a shirt!

[![](/assets/images/posts/i_made_a_ficlet/2312957997_29c1dbbec8.jpg)](http://flickr.com/photos/kplawver/2312957997/)

Here's how I did it:

1. Printed out the "f" from the ficlets logo.
2. Cut it out
3. Traced it on the shirt with an orange sharpie.
4. Using a _very_ fine brush, I painted the logo with bleach.

That's it! I think it turned out OK. I plan on wearing it to the awards ceremony Sunday night. Hopefully, it'll be bring good luck!
