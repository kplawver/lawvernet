It's started! For two months, I'll be shaving every day, except for my mustache "area". Yes, [The Great Stache Off](http://thegreatstacheoff.com) has begun! I have a goal of raising two thousand bucks for the [Sean Kimerling Testicular Cancer Foundation](http://seankimerling.org/new_site/new_pages/about_us/about_foundation.php) () in two months. I've never really done anything like this, but, I think the idea is so funny, and such a great way to raise money, that I couldn't resist.\\ So... if you've got some spare dough, why not help a nut out? If for nothing else, so [Cletus](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kplawver/159592653/) can make a comeback!\\ (the possibilities for puns and naughtiness for the next two months are endless... expect lots and lots of ball jokes)\\ **Update**: [I'll be doing daily photo updates over on Flickr.](http://www.flickr.com/photos/kplawver/sets/72157601799509224/) You can follow along (yes, day one is kind of pathetic, but I'm not a yeti... it'll take a good week before it looks like anything)!\\
