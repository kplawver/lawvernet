Being on the governing board at [Susie King Taylor Community School](https://www.sktcs.org) has been an education. We had board training about consensus-based decision making recently and something our facilitator said keeps bouncing around in my head: _building consensus is hard up front, but makes what comes later easier because everyone was a part of coming to the decision. Leading by command is easy up front, but makes what comes after harder, because you don't have the community's agreement._
