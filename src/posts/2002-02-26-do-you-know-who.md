Do you know who Bela Fleck is? Should you? Laws, yes you should! He and the [Flecktones](http://www.flecktones.com) have been creating amazing music for about ten years now, and show no signs of slowing down. They've broken all the laws of their instruments and man, you should just go out right now and buy **Flight of the Cosmic Hippo**, **Live Art**, **Tales From the Acoustic Planet** and **Live at the Quick** right now and melt into music bliss.

Other than that, I have nothing to say today that I haven't said already. Work is really busy and it's sapping all my creativity.
