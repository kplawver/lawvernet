[I love it that the President wants some ribs](http://www.whitehouse.gov/news/releases/2004/01/20040122-5.html "Remarks by the President to the Press Pool"). I'm not one to deny a man his ribs, but could the President answer one question? What's the point of posting it to the White House website when he says absolutely nothing of substance? Was it that Karl wasn't there to give him the answers? One of the most disconcerting things about the President is his inability to answer a question off the top of his head without sounding like a chronic stutterer. Some of his biggest gaffes have come when he's not reading froma script. If I were to vote for anyone in his Administration, it would be his speechwriter, who apparently is quite eloquent. The President? Not so much.
