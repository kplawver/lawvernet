I've mentioned it before, but [Episode 79 of Radio Clash](http://www.mutantpop.net/radioclash/archives/2006/06/28/rc-79/) is the best podcast ever. Tim's put together a retrospective of early mashup/bootleg remixes, and it's priceless. If you've ever been interested in what mashups are all about, this single podcast will turn you on and blow you away. **Owner of a Lonely Butt** will make you shake it and laugh it off all at the same time (and **enya vs. prodigy** is great too... I remember listening to that a _long_ time ago, nice to have it back).\\ Bravo, Mr. Bearcub, bravo.
