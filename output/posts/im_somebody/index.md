date: 2004-07-09

For the longest time, Google thought I was a typo. When you searched for "[Kevin Lawver](http://www.google.com/search?q=Kevin Lawver&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8 "Google Search: Kevin Lawver ")", it used to tell me that I was nobody. It used to tell everyone that they really meant "Kevin Lawlor", whoever that is. But, no longer!! I am somebody now!! We've been using me as the spellcheck query at work long enough that Google's figured out that I really exist, and "Lawver" is spelled correctly (how sad is it when the entire world, including major search engines, misspell your last name?). Now, when you search for [me](http://www.google.com/search?q=Kevin Lawver&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8), I am me, and Google doesn't think you've made a mistake. Heck, if you [misspell me](http://www.google.com/search?q=Kevin Lawve&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8), it tries to point you back to me! How cool is that?

This totally makes my day. Thanks to Franco for pointing it out.
