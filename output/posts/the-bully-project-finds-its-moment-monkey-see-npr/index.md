I was always a big kid, but that didn't keep me from getting bullied at school, usually picked on because of my weight or that I was a Mormon (and usually the only one in my grade). Thankfully, it stopped by high school (probably because I was, again, big). I'm glad to see that there's a documentary out there like [The Bully Project](http://www.npr.org/blogs/monkeysee/2011/06/23/137362129/the-bully-project-finds-its-moment?ft=1&f=93568166). Bullying is horrible, and too often it's ignored by schools and misunderstood by parents.

I don't know how to stop it... but talking about it can only help.
