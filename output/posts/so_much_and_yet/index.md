There's so much I want to ask you, Internet Pals, but absolutely nothing I can write here. Why? It would definitely break [the rules](/archive/2003/02/11/h16_the_ethics_of_blogging.php). But, I just have to say, I've never wanted to break the rules more than I do right now. Pretty much everything going on in my life at the moment would require me to break those rules (no embarrassing family stories, nothing that would get me fired, etc), and well, I don't want to make anything worse than it already is.\\ Things aren't bad. In a lot of ways, things are very very good. Things are just complicated. The more complicated things get, the more I want to write about them, which would just further complicate them.\\ I can tell you that I'm going to Dublin in the very near future, Boston after that (to hang out with my fellow standards nerds at the W3C), and then to Austin for Geek Summer Camp (you know, [SXSW](http://www.sxsw.com). Frequent flier miles are mine, all mine, baby.\\ And maybe while I'm in dark, cold and super-friendly Dublin, I'll tell you a little about all the fun stuff happening at Church, because I know you're dying to know. You all are, I just know it.
