[Joe drops out](http://www.sfgate.com/cgi-bin/article.cgi?f=/news/archive/2004/02/03/politics2058EST0852.DTL). I'm happy. Lieberman came off as a whiner and a scold during the campaign, and honestly, during the debates, sounded like a Republican. He just didn't click with me, or with anyone I know. I feel bad for the folks who support him, but I'm glad the field's narrowing. Now that Joe's out, I think it's time for Dennis and Al to look at the facts and drop out as well.

Now if only we can get GW to drop out of the race too!
